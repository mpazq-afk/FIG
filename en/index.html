Forging Tomorrow's Leaders.

FEN Investment Group (FIG) is the student-run investment community of the Faculty of Economics and Business at Universidad de Chile. We train the next generation of investors through hands-on portfolio management, industry visits, open education and a flagship portfolio tournament run on Bloomberg terminals.

How we got here

Built by students, in two years.

FIG started from a simple conviction: financial markets are not understood from the sidelines, they are learned by managing them. A group of students set out to go one step past the syllabus, and the club grew from there.

Flagship event

Portfolio Tournament 2026.

Fifty-four student teams manage simulated USD 10 million portfolios invested in iShares/BlackRock ETFs, benchmarked against the MSCI ACWI and scored on Bloomberg PORT metrics, Information Ratio, excess return, Sharpe, VaR and maximum drawdown. Rankings are published every Friday; the top five teams defend their management before an expert jury at the final on November 9, 2026.

Not only portfolios

Two more competitions, same year.

Valuation Challenge 2026

One month, one real M&A deal

Teams analyse an actual cross-border M&A transaction end to end, build the models and defend their thesis in a final pitch before a jury of Investment Banking leaders from Itaú, with BDO Deal Advisory. First place earns an internship at Itaú; second, at BDO.

Trading Tournament 2026

National, one month

Open to students across Chile, competing on technical analysis, risk management and execution. XTB is the strategic sponsor, providing an institutional-grade platform.
